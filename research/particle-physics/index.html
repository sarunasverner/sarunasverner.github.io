Particle physics

Particle physics & field theory

What do field dynamics and symmetry reveal about physics beyond the Standard Model?

Particle physics is a central part of my research program. I investigate nonperturbative electroweak configurations, scattering amplitudes in supergravity and models that connect inflation with supersymmetry breaking and dark matter. These problems require attention to both the mathematical structure of a theory and the physical processes it predicts.

My calculations range from analytical field-theory methods to numerical solutions of coupled nonlinear equations. I am particularly interested in problems where resolving the dynamics changes our understanding of a production mechanism, a consistency condition or a possible observable signature.

Selected contributions

Questions, calculations and results.

  • Electroweak sphalerons

    With Konstantin Matchev, I revisited the static sphaleron, its energy barrier and unstable mode, and followed its nonlinear decay. The two studies connect the structure of an electroweak field configuration to its evolution and particle spectra.

    Read the paper, arXiv:2505.05607

  • From equations to public software

    The companion decay study analyzes the evolving gauge and Higgs fields under different initial conditions. A public C solver and Mathematica notebook are available through the sphaleron-decay repository.

    Read the paper, arXiv:2505.05608

  • Scattering in curved field space

    With collaborators, I calculated supergravity scattering amplitudes using Riemann and Kähler normal coordinates. Comparing these approaches clarifies how the background and field-space geometry enter the calculation.

    Read the paper, arXiv:2302.05456

  • No-scale supergravity and model building

    My work explores no-scale constructions that connect inflation, modulus stabilization, supersymmetry breaking and dark energy, as well as the production and phenomenology of gravitinos and other dark-sector candidates.

    Read the paper, arXiv:1903.05267

Where this leads

This field-theory foundation informs my current work on gravitational production in supergravity cosmology and the use of machine learning to explore constrained theory spaces.
