About

I work across cosmology and particle physics, studying how the dynamics of fundamental fields shape the universe and how their signatures can be tested.

I joined the Kavli Institute for Cosmological Physics at the University of Chicago in September 2025. Previously, I was a postdoctoral researcher at the University of Florida’s Institute for Fundamental Theory. I received my Ph.D. in physics from the University of Minnesota in 2022, working with Keith Olive on no-scale supergravity, inflation and dark matter.

My earlier training was at the University of Southampton, where I completed a BSc and an MPhys in Physics with Astronomy. As part of the MPhys program, I carried out research at the Harvard-Smithsonian Center for Astrophysics with Belinda Wilkes. I was born in Vilnius, Lithuania, and my academic path has taken me through the United Kingdom and the United States.

Mentoring

Research grows through shared calculations, sustained discussion and the development of new researchers.

Cosmological particle production

With Jingyuan Wang and Rocky Kolb, I have worked on gravitational particle production in multifield inflation, connecting field dynamics during inflation to the origin of dark matter. Read the paper

Gravitational waves from cosmic strings

Work with Christos Litos and collaborators investigates gravitational waves from long strings and loops. Read the preprint

Machine-learned symmetries

I supervised or co-supervised Roy Forestano, Alex Roman and Eyup Unlu on work relating machine learning to continuous symmetries and Lie-algebra structure. The collaboration produced four papers in 2023, one in Machine Learning: Science and Technology and three in Physics Letters B.

Teaching

At the University of Florida, I was a guest instructor for undergraduate Classical Mechanics and the graduate Standard Model course. At the University of Minnesota, I was a teaching assistant for introductory physics, undergraduate Quantum Mechanics and graduate Advanced Quantum Mechanics, and received the Outstanding Teaching Assistant Award in 2020.
